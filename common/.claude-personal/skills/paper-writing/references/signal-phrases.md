# Signal-phrase bank

A quick-grab list of the rhetorical moves that recur across the worked examples, grouped by what they accomplish. Use them while drafting to perform a move cleanly. They are patterns, not mandatory wording; adapt to context and avoid repeating the same phrase within a paper.

## Open a topic and assert importance

- "X is a hallmark phenomenon of ..."
- "X is a fundamental problem in ..."
- "X is a widely used method for ..."
- "X has been the powerhouse of ... in the last decade."
- "The recent emergence of ... calls for the necessity of ..."

## Contextualize / show where efforts concentrate

- "A well-known recipe for X involves ..."
- "The consensus is that ..."
- "Extensive research has shown that ..."
- "Among the many ... paradigms, X has proven ..."
- "Design and deployment of ... has seen a lot of progress ..."

## Mark a gap (the hinge)

- "However, previous works study almost only ..." (however + almost only)
- "However, these methods only ..." (however + only = room for improvement)
- "However, to our knowledge, no studies have ..." (to our knowledge = scoop protection)
- "the direction that remains largely unexplored is ..."
- "Unfortunately, none of these efforts have been done in the direction of ..."
- "how to design ... remains an open problem."

## State motivation, objective, hypothesis, or research question

- "Motivated by these findings, we aim to investigate ..."
- "Following these findings, we hypothesize that ..."
- "This raises the question, what happens in ...?"
- "To investigate, we trained ..."
- "Our goal is to ..."

## Introduce the proposed work

- "In this work, we propose and explore ..."
- "Here we develop ..."
- "To address these gaps, we formulate a novel ..."
- "To bridge this gap, we take a step towards ..."

## Phrase contributions as insights (lead with a finding verb)

- "We observe ..."
- "We introduce ..."
- "We show that ..."
- "We conceptualize ..."
- "We demonstrate ..."
- Anti-pattern: "We conduct experiments on dataset X" by itself. Always complete it: "... and we show that ...".

## Mark the difference from related work (mandatory after any related mention)

- "We distinguish ourselves from these papers by ..."
- "In contrast, we focus on ..."
- "Unlike existing ..., our method ..."
- "In comparison to our approach, ..."
- "The most notable difference between [X] and our work is ..."
- Closing advantage after a series of contrasts (stacks one more point in your favor): "In addition, [their work] has not demonstrated ..."

## Flag concurrent work

- "Concurrent with our work, ..."
- "Concurrent Works. ..."
- "We note that there is a concurrent ..."

## Group works and contrast with the category

- "X has been long studied under various conditions (citations). In particular, [one representative work] provides ..."
- "Techniques for X are mostly focused on [category] (citations)."
- "[Category] methods traditionally perform ... Unlike existing [category] methods, our ..."
- "While these works provide ..., our analysis leads to ..."

## Justify a setup or methodological choice via the literature

- "Following [ref], we use ..."
- "using two popular ... architectures ..."
- "a simple and popular approach to ..."
- "all subsequent papers have used ... Consequently, we largely focus on ..."

## Open a problem-definition / formalism

- "Given a [object] T = {...}, the objective of [task] is to ..."
- "Let X in R^{n x d} denote ... We are interested in ..."
- "Formally, our goal is to find ..."
- "We assume to be given ..." (constraints)
- "We consider three types of tasks: ..."

## Conclude an experiment (never end on a table pointer)

- "Results ... are presented in Table N. We find that ..."
- "The obtained results highlight ..."
- "The main takeaway. ..."
- "Results of all settings in Figure N provide evidence to support our ..."
- Anti-pattern: ending with "Table N shows the results."

## Corroborate or refute the literature

- "This is surprising and contradictory to the previous findings (citations) ..."
- "This result concurs with the observations made by ..."
- "This view aligns with the observation from Figure N ..."
- "This observation aligns with our earlier findings suggesting that ..."

## Explain a figure (say what to notice, do not echo the caption)

- "In the left column of Figure N, we observe that ..."
- "Two observations can be obtained from the plots: 1) ... 2) ..."
- "Figure N reveals that ..."
